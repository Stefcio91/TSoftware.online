                  Scenario
                  
It's 11:40 pm. An order comes in. Nobody's in the office.

                  
Scroll down and the automation will run through its steps one by one. Scroll back up to rewind.

                

                

                  Step 1 · trigger
                  
An order comes in

                  
A customer clicks in the shop or sends an email. A webhook catches it within half a second.

                

                

                  Step 2 · AI
                  
AI checks that everything adds up

                  
VAT ID against the register, the address, whether it's a duplicate. If something looks off, it sets the order aside for a human.

                

                

                  Step 3 · ERP
                  
The document creates itself

                  
Line items, prices and stock levels go straight into Comarch, SAP or Subiekt. Nobody retypes anything.

                

                

                  Step 4 · action
                  
The invoice goes out to the customer

                  
PDF, email, text message. The document lands in the right folder, no searching needed.

                

                

                  Step 5 · team
                  
The sales rep only gets what matters

                  
A short note on Teams or Slack with a link. Exceptions wait for a single click in the morning.

                

                

                  Result
                  
Human work: 26 min

                  
That's how long it took by hand, and that's what's left after automation. At 10 orders a day that's roughly 20 hours a week.

FAQ

          
Common questions

          
Can't find your question? Write to us, we answer everything.

        

        

          

            Does this even make sense for a small business?
            
It does, and often it makes the most sense there. In a small company one person juggles five things at once, so every hour you take off their plate is worth a lot. We start with whatever pays back fastest.

          

          

            Do I have to replace my software?
            
No. We connect what you already use: your ERP, email, spreadsheets, CRM, online shop. We only add a new tool when something is genuinely missing.

          

          

            What about GDPR and my data?
            
Minimal access rights, a data processing agreement, and sensitive data can stay on your side. Only what the task needs ever reaches the AI.

          

          

            How much does it cost?
            
It depends on what we build. Simple automations take a few days of work, bigger integrations are quoted in stages. With every quote you get a second number next to it: how much you'll save.

          

          

            When will I see results?
            
The first automations are usually running within a few days. Bigger projects go live in pieces, so every stage delivers something right away.

          

          

            What if something breaks?
            
The automations are monitored, so we get an alert before you notice anything. As part of support we handle changes in your systems, APIs and data formats.
